Privacy Policy

Updated: November 8, 2026

Buddhist Edu. supports administrators, teachers and students with school years, temples, classes, student records, photos, documents and notifications.

1. Scope

Buddhist Edu. is a Buddhist education management application for administrators, teachers and students. This policy covers the data processed through the application.

2. Information processed

Account information may include email, name and role. Student records may include name, school year, temple, class, portrait photographs and related documents. The service also processes class invitations, approvals, notifications and operation records.

3. Why we use it

We process information to authenticate users, manage classes and student records, review photographs, export authorized data and deliver notifications. Student photos are not deliberately made public through unrestricted sharing links.

4. Service providers and storage

Supabase handles authentication, structured records, permissions and notifications. Google Drive holds photographs and documents. Older files may remain in Supabase Storage. Data may be processed in other countries where providers operate.

5. Access and security

Students can view permitted personal records; teachers work within assigned classes; each administrator unit is logically separated. The owner of the connected Google Drive account can administer its stored files. Access controls operate on the server.

6. Retention and requests

Records are kept as necessary for educational administration and in accordance with the responsible organization's policies. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable retention and approval requirements. Administrators can export ZIP archives.

7. Student and minor information

Education providers and teachers must have an appropriate basis to collect student information and images, especially for minors. Upload only material necessary for the educational purpose.

8. Contact and changes

For privacy requests, contact watompangsane84@gmail.com. This policy may be updated as app features or data handling practices change.
